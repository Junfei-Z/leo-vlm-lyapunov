\subsection{Scaling the Source Set: All 45 Classes}\label{EV_SRC45}
We extend the six-source mainline to all 45 RESISC45 classes with zero
selection: the measured per-class accuracy table enters the simulation raw
(no merging, no rounding), and the per-source arrival rate is scaled to
$0.10 \times 6/45$ so the total offered load matches the mainline -- the
source structure is the only moved variable. Nine classes are structurally
unservable: their best model accuracy lies below the per-arrival target
($\max_m Q_{im} < U^{\mathrm{tgt}} = 0.30$), so their fairness queues cannot
be stabilized by any scheduler at any service rate. (A pre-registered
gap-based threshold was invalidated by the continuous, non-bimodal accuracy
spectrum; we adopt this model-grounded criterion, which predates all
45-source data.) Raw min-fill is 0.000 for every policy -- pinned by the
unservable classes, it degenerates into an indicator of their existence --
so the informative metrics are the fill quantiles and the min-fill over the
36 servable classes.

The fine-grained source set restores the fairness differentiation that the
six-source mainline compresses. Among the deployable policies the proposed
scheduler is the only one that keeps every servable class alive
(servable-set min-fill $0.058$ at $V{=}10$, against $0.000$ for both
Greedy-E and Static): the simple policies starve servable classes
structurally, not accidentally -- Greedy-E serves everything with the
cheapest model and any class with 2B accuracy near zero receives no
quality; Static serves everything with 3B and starves the three servable
classes whose 3B accuracy is at most 0.04 (among them the split-dependent
rectangular\_farmland, which the proposed scheduler sustains through the 7B
pipeline). The reckless splitters reach higher servable-set min-fill
(Greedy-Q 0.149, Random 0.081) but remain undeployable here as everywhere
(6.2\% and 5.9\% downtime). The protection has its usual price: the
proposed scheduler's median fill (0.183) and total quality (1580) sit below
the simple policies' (Static 0.314/3777), the fairness--throughput conflict
at fine granularity. One boundary case documents what the servability
criterion does and does not classify: palace ($\max_m Q = 0.28$, just below
$U^{\mathrm{tgt}}$) is unservable yet not unserved -- its unstable queue
keeps demanding, and the max-min scheduler keeps feeding it service that
cannot reach the target (fill 0.071). The criterion classifies queue
stabilizability, not service.

% ---- consistency note with the 6-source mainline (one sentence, honest) ----
The six-source finding that Static leads deployable min-fill at the
rule-sized point does not extend to fine granularity: it depended on every
mainline class being 3B-viable (the six classes' 3B accuracies are all
$\ge 0.33$), a property the full class set does not have.
